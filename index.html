My research lies at the intersection of Robot Manipulation, and Robotic Foundation Model, with a broader interest in Embodied Intelligence, Robot Leaning, and Robotics. I am currently pursuing my PhD degree in the Robotics Institute at Beihang University (BUAA). Before that, I received my B.S. degree from the School of Mechatronics Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), Harbin, China, and my M.S. degree from the School of Astronautics, Beihang University, Beijing, China.
